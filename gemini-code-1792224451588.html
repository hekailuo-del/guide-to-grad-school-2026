Words to Know

Stipend

A regular fixed allowance paid to a student to cover basic living expenses (housing, food, transport) during their studies.

Tuition Waiver

An agreement where the university or scholarship body forgives 100% or a percentage of your academic tuition fees.

Apostille / Legalization

Official embassy or government authentication verifying that your degree certificate and transcript are genuine for international use.

Letter of Expected Graduation

An official document from your university registrar confirming you are in your final year and scheduled to graduate before master's enrollment.
